Discount Calculator — Final Price and Amount Saved

A discount calculator turns a sale price into a clear answer in seconds: how much you save, and what you actually pay.

What this tool does

Enter an original price and a discount percentage, and the tool shows the amount saved and the price you pay. It is useful in shops, for online sales, and for checking whether a “deal” really is one.

How to use it, step by step

  1. Type the full original price, before any discount.
  2. Type the discount as a number, for example 25 for 25%.
  3. Press Calculate.
  4. Compare the saving and the final price before deciding to buy.

Worked example

Shoes cost 120 with 30% off. The saving is 120 × 30 ÷ 100 = 36. The final price is 120 − 36 = 84. If a second shop offers the same shoes at 90 with no discount, the first shop is still cheaper, even though 90 sounds simple.

Common mistakes

  • Applying the discount twice: a further 10% off an already discounted price is 10% of the lower price, not 10% of the original.
  • Ignoring extra costs: delivery, tax or fees can change the real total, so check the checkout total too.
  • Assuming a bigger percentage is always better: 50% off an inflated starting price can cost more than a smaller discount on a fair price.

Frequently asked questions

How is the final price worked out?

Final price = original price − (original price × discount ÷ 100).

What if the discount is over 100%?

That would mean the seller pays you, which is not a normal sale. Check the numbers if you see a result below zero.

Does this include tax?

No. This tool calculates the discount only. Add tax separately if it applies in your country or shop.

Can I use it for two discounts in a row?

Yes, but in two steps: use the final price from the first calculation as the original price for the second.
